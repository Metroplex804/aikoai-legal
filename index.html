Privacy by design

Clear choices. No surprises.

Your voice powers the conversation

Microphone audio is processed to provide AikoAI's realtime voice experience. AikoAI does not claim to store raw audio recordings.

Memory is explained

Conversation text, summaries and selected memories may be retained so future conversations can be more helpful and personalised.

Deletion is available

You can initiate permanent account deletion in the app. Subscription cancellation is handled separately through Apple.
