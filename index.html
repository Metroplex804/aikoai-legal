What AikoAI processes

AikoAI uses microphone audio to provide realtime conversation. Conversation text, summaries and selected memories may be retained so future conversations can be more useful. Raw microphone recordings are not claimed to be stored as audio files.

Realtime voice

Microphone audio is processed to understand speech and generate spoken replies.

Persistent memory

Selected conversation information may be retained to support continuity across sessions.

Account control

You can initiate permanent account deletion in the app. Apple subscription cancellation remains separate.
