Delete your AikoAI account

You can initiate permanent deletion from within AikoAI. This page explains the steps, what is removed and how Apple subscriptions are handled.

How to initiate deletion

  1. Open AikoAI and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open the menu, then choose Account.
  3. Choose Delete Account and read the deletion warning.
  4. Complete the deliberate confirmation shown in the app.
  5. AikoAI will begin the permanent account-deletion process and sign you out when it completes.
Deletion is permanent. You will lose access to your profile, conversation information, memories and remaining usage associated with that AikoAI account. This cannot be undone.

What deletion removes

Deletion is designed to remove information associated with your AikoAI account from the active service, including:

  • your AikoAI profile and learning preferences;
  • stored conversation turns and conversation summaries;
  • persistent memories extracted from your conversations;
  • active AikoAI voice-session and usage associations where deletion is operationally and legally appropriate;
  • your Supabase authentication account; and
  • local AikoAI session and pending-preference information on the device.

If a live voice conversation is active, AikoAI will end it before deletion proceeds. Sign in with Apple authorisation associated with the AikoAI account will be revoked where required.

Information that may be retained for a limited purpose

We may retain the minimum information reasonably required to comply with legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, security, dispute or enforcement obligations. Apple and RevenueCat may separately retain transaction and subscription records under their own legal obligations and privacy policies. Information retained for these limited purposes is not kept in the active AikoAI profile for continued personalisation.

Account deletion does not cancel your Apple subscription

Important: deleting AikoAI and cancelling Aiko Monthly are separate actions. Deletion does not stop an App Store subscription from renewing.

Before or after deleting your account, cancel Aiko Monthly through:

  1. iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → AikoAI → Cancel Subscription; or
  2. Apple's subscription-management page.

Apple controls App Store billing, cancellation and refund eligibility.

If you cannot access the app

If you cannot sign in or reach the account-deletion control, email help.metrolabs@gmail.com with the subject “AikoAI account deletion request”.

For security, we will need to verify that you control the account. Do not email your Apple password, sign-in codes or payment details. Support-assisted deletion may take longer than the in-app process because identity must be checked.

Before you delete

  • Finish or end any active voice conversation.
  • Cancel the Apple subscription separately if you do not want it to renew.
  • Understand that saved conversation context and memories cannot be restored after deletion.
  • If you only want to stop using AikoAI temporarily, you may sign out instead.

For more information about processing and your legal rights, read the Privacy Policy.
